    💡 What is IDEA//BANK?

    Your head is noisy. IDEA//BANK is a quiet place to catch each idea or thought as it turns up, decide what it's worth in one quick pass, and act on that: bank the good ones, park the maybes, and bin the rest. Once a thought has a home, your mind can stop carrying it.

    🏦 Bank

    Ideas worth keeping, filed under the part of life they serve: Mind, Soul, Body, Financial, Career.

    🅿️ Parking

    Not sure yet? Park it. It waits without nagging, and you can pull it back into the loop whenever you want.

    🗑️ Trash

    ♻️ Recyclable: a negative thought you can rework into something useful. ✕ Useless: let it go for good.

    🧠 Why this helps

    IDEA//BANK borrows a few moves from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), one of the most studied talk-therapy toolkits. It isn't therapy, but these small habits are the everyday version of what CBT practises.

    • ✍️ Get it out of your head. An unfinished thought keeps tugging for attention, an old finding known as the Zeigarnik effect. Writing it into a place you trust to revisit lets your mind stop rehearsing it.
    • 🏷️ Name it to tame it. Tagging a thought and filing it puts a step between you and it. CBT calls this externalizing: you begin to see a thought as a thought, not a fact.
    • ♻️ Reframe instead of ruminate. The Recyclable bin is a tiny thought record. Instead of looping on "I never finish anything," you catch it, weigh it, and rework it into something fairer. CBT's aim isn't happy thoughts, it's accurate ones.
    • 🅿️ Park the maybes. Putting an undecided thought off until later is "worry postponement," a common CBT step that eases the pressure to decide right now, so one thought stops owning the hour.
    • 🗑️ Let the useless go. Not every thought earns your time. Choosing to bin one is a small, deliberate act of letting go.
    • 🎯 See where your head goes. Sorting ideas by life area shows you where your attention actually lands, so you can spend it on purpose.

    ✅ What it is

    • A fast catcher for ideas and passing thoughts
    • A calm place to sort, keep, and let go
    • A CBT-inspired daily habit
    • Private by default: everything stays in your browser
    • A mirror that shows your patterns over time

    🚫 What it isn't

    • Not therapy, and not a substitute for a professional
    • Not a diagnosis or a medical tool
    • Not a to-do list or task manager
    • Not a vault that keeps everything forever
    • Not a crisis service

    🔒 Your data & privacy

    • 📵 It stays on your device. Every idea is saved only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded, because there is no server and no account.
    • 🧩 Encoded, not encrypted. Your data is stored encoded so it isn't plain text at a glance, but anyone who can use this browser profile can read it. Treat it as private notes on your own device, not a locked vault.
    • 🌐 The page is public, your data isn't. The app's code is served from a public web address, but it ships empty. What you type never leaves your browser.
    • 🧹 You're in control. Clearing your browser data, or the "Clear" button, erases everything. There is no copy anywhere else, so keep your own backup if it matters to you.

    💙 This is a self-help tool, not a clinician. If your thoughts feel overwhelming or unsafe, please reach out to a mental-health professional or a local crisis line. You deserve real support, not just an app.
